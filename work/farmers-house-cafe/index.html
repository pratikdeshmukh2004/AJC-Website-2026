Creative Direction

There is always one café from our travels
that lingers in the mind long after we leave.

Farmers’ House is a heartfelt tribute to the generational farmers of Turtuk. It honors the custodians of the land who breathed life into Turtuk’s barren soil through centuries-old agricultural practices, transforming the rugged region into a self-sustaining land of rich vegetation and life.

As you step inside the café, entering a world of fine coffee and finer cuisine, you will spot a few charming oddities: a rustic ledge where the espresso bar now sits, and legacy farming tools on display – all belonging to the very family that cultivated this corner in Ladakh’s last village.

Identity

The farmers themselves form the identity and the core of the branding brief. The silhouette of the logo draws inspiration
from the rugged village homes of Turtuk, honoring their raw, non-linear outlines. Complementing this form are delicate flower buds that symbolize life and prosperity – a visual metaphor for resilience and renewal. The entire palette embodies this living heritage, utilizing warm beige and deep brown tones to pay tribute to the old stone-and-mud houses of Turtuk; shelters that nurtured the roots of this café and the generations behind it.

Colour Palette

Menu Design

Cultivated from the past to serve travelers of the present, the menu adopts a minimalist design structured entirely around the ingredients of the land and speciality coffee.

Where earthen plates rest and endless coffee pours, the café fills with the gentle notes of jazz and blues – a soundscape echoing into the vastness, offering the comforting homeliness of returning to familiar habits in a distant land. Communicated to the world through words, photographs, visuals, and music.
