Lahé Life

Brand Identity Narrative

Lahé Life is a conscious collection defined by the philosophy of the slow and the stewardship of the land. Born from the Assamese way of life where time moves gently, it is a crossover of heritage hospitality with the modern sensibility of the present day.

With roots in a forty-year legacy of exploration, the brand pairs a deep-seated regional soul with a collection of ventures that feel familiar and easy to return to. From the unhurried journeys of Assam Bengal Navigation along the Ganges and Brahmaputra rivers to the immersive sanctuary on land of Diphlu River Lodge, every experience is shaped by a quiet intimacy with the surroundings.

Identity

Iconography

The Logo draws inspiration from young bamboo shoots and river reeds, where multiple stems rise from a shared root system. It reflects a quiet, continuous growth that is resilient and deeply connected to its origin. Each form grows upward with intention, suggesting expansion while remaining anchored, much like the different brands within Lahé Life that evolve individually yet belong to a larger whole.

The softness of the lines keeps the symbol organic, while its structure brings a sense of balance and clarity. It is not a literal depiction, but an interpretation of growth as a living system, one that is grounded, adaptive, and enduring.

Colour Palette

Slowly, by Nature
Our palette is an echo of the landscape, moving slowly by nature.
Browns and greys anchor the brand in the quiet weight of soil and stone – grounded, tactile, and enduring. In the spaces between, blue and ivory white introduce movement, drawing from the breath of open water and the clarity of unhurried air. Muted greens complete the narrative, reflecting the forests and the layered wilderness that reveals itself softly, one detail at a time.

Together, these tones form a visual language that is felt before it is seen, rooted deeply in the place and its slowness.
