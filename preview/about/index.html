In Bhutan

There are journeys that transform human perspective; As you descend into the land of the Thunder Dragon, whose belly has the slow-moving towns of Bhutan, you enter the world’s last Shangri-La—protected by Himalayan mountains and dense forests. Here, the 21st century comes to a hold and Buddhist prophecies still live in the air. Here, a life of balance is their spirituality.

In Ladakh

Our home for all of two years, Ladakh is an ancient land in the shadows of the Himalayas and the Karakoram. It is where one feels small in front of the mountains, and giant in the realm of possibilities. Some say, it is the last wilderness left wild, each relic still breathing, from the generations that traversed these mountains long before us. As keepers of history, and tellers of story, we entered Ladakh to never leave.

In Punjab

Punjab is soul—it is Khatirdari, a spirit that loosely translates to absolute hospitality and warmth for guests. As you walk through, you find what we call 'alive archives'— in local people, some old stories, folk music, and purani Havelis where history is as present as shared laughter and a lot of food. That feeling of coming home is native to here, and it is one place where we truly felt ‘taken care of’.

ranjit’s svaasa

In Assam

Assam is the wild heart of eastern India, that can be entered through land, through water, through loom and through the wild. Under its dense green canopy and along the Brahmaputra's sweeping banks, time softens. Tea is sipped slowly, always out of a kettle. Life is lived patiently, often in coexistence, where birds and a gentle breeze carry the forests across the land.

laheké cafe

In Bengal

Most people look at Bengal and see soil; we looked at the Hooghly and saw a living, breathing artery. On those river banks, life unfolds without a stage—raw, rooted, and completely indifferent to modern speed. We had never seen how differently waterways move before, carrying daily rituals, old trade routes, and silent dignity, that now reflects in our visuals of them.

assam bengal navigation

In Delhi

Delhi is our cultural chaos, our curiosity. Home to many communities and India’s greatest historic events, it is a city that reciprocates love in its own kind ways. Walking the soul of Purani Dilli’s labyrinthine lanes, the said chaos led us to Dharampura, where Mughal history resides in a 200-year-old Haveli. Century-old locals still tell its story, opening a world of living history within their own.

haveli dharampura

In Rajasthan

In Rajasthan, royalty was once a rule of India, written into fort walls, golden sands, and generations of lineage. Standing within architecture that has witnessed centuries turn, we felt the monumental weight of living history that refuses to become a memory. Here, heritage survives, as does the hospitality, now shaped regally, for the modern traveler.
