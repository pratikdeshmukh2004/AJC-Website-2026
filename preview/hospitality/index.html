Who We Are

AJC has been our way of telling stories.

We began in the hospitality industry in 2024 with a foundation in corporate business and strategy. For our first hotel launch, we set out for a year-long residency in India’s Himalayan region of Ladakh, marking a decisive pivot and the launch of AJC Hospitality, grounded in a single realisation:

“What guests feel when they think of a hotel is just as vital as the hotel itself.”

It is the human touch, the human story, that people truly travel for.

We believe small luxury hotels around the world deserve that crucial level of intentionality.

When you invest deeply in your narrative rather than selling generic room nights, guests feel invited into a rare cultural fabric from their very first interaction.

If your hotel feels like anywhere in the world, and nowhere special, your guests may never return.

For this, we enter your journey.

We are not traditional hospitality consultants; we are industry tastemakers.

Informed by our global travels and deep engagement with cultural communities, we create worlds within yours.

From the foundational vision that inspired your hotel to its ever-evolving brand perception, we are present at every step.

We launch new hotels and re-brand legacy ones.

Our Process

We step into the critical window between construction and operations - and sometimes years later.

De-briefing with Core Stories

Before any visual or experiential work begins, we unlock the foundational narrative of the property. Through our 4-stage Steel Box debriefing process, we excavate the principal’s original vision, the architectural intent, and the deeper cultural roots of the region to isolate the property’s singular, defensible story.

Living Residency & Audit

We do not believe in designing luxury from a distance; to build something meaningful in a place, we live on shared land alongside your team and community. We audit the world through a sensory and narrative lens, evaluating every micro-interaction.

Identity & Experience Design

With the residency insights established, we craft the core brand identity, narrative, visual language, culinary framework, and guest journey rituals. We bring together the creative teams of photographers, filmmakers, locals and more, to birth a brand directly from its land.

Digital Development

We translate the physical atmosphere of the hotel into its flagship digital platform and communication assets. Finally, we establish the PR guidelines, social media setup, and launch strategy required to unveil a hotel to the curated global audience of tastemakers and guests.
